Take-off Performance References

Sources for accelerate-stop distance, one-engine-inoperative take-off distance and balanced take-off decision speed for a 19-seat electric aircraft.

Page references identify printed page numbers and, where different, one-based PDF page positions. Torenbeek's printed pages are 20 lower than the PDF page positions. Hoogreef's printed page 19 is PDF page 20. Web sources have no fixed page numbers and are identified by section, table or equation.

The adopted values, reference-aircraft estimates and additional modelling assumptions are distinguished below. Background comparisons are identified separately from parameters used in the calculation.
